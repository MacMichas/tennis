# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy covers {{ site.title }} for Apple Watch and its iPhone companion.

## In short

- {{ site.title }} has no accounts and no server. What it records stays on your Apple Watch and your iPhone.
- It shows no ads, sells nothing, and contains no analytics or tracking tools.
- Something leaves your devices only in the cases below: when you export sessions, when the app shows or opens a session's place on a map, and when you test the app through TestFlight.

## What the app records

During a session, on your Apple Watch:

- **Your swings.** The watch's motion sensors run while a session runs, so the app can recognise your forehands and backhands. The movement is analysed on the watch, and for each swing the app keeps what it found: the stroke, when it happened, and measurements such as how fast it was. It keeps no recording of your movement.
- **Your workout, if you allow it.** The app saves each session to Apple Health as a tennis workout, and reads your heart rate and the energy you burn while it runs. The session keeps your average and latest heart rate, the energy and the distance. You can stop saving sessions to Health in the app's Settings on the watch.
- **Where the session started, if you allow it.** With location allowed for the app on your iPhone, the watch takes one reading of where it is as a session starts and keeps it with that session, so you can see where you played. The app never follows your location during a session or at any other time.
- **The session itself:** when it started, how long it lasted, and whether it was training or a match.

The app also keeps your setup: which wrist wears the watch, which hand holds the racket, and your daily goal.

## Where it stays

- Sessions are stored by the app on your Apple Watch and copied to your paired iPhone, over the connection Apple provides between the two. What you set in the app, such as your daily goal, moves between them the same way.
- They stay there until you delete them or the app.
- The app keeps nothing on a server and stores nothing in iCloud itself. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes the app's data, as it does for any app.
- Workouts saved to Health are kept by Apple Health, under its own protection.

## When something leaves your devices

- **When you export.** You can export sessions from the iPhone as files and share them wherever you choose. An export holds your sessions as the app records them, except where they were played: that never leaves in an export.
- **Maps.** To show where a session was played and name the place, the iPhone app asks Apple Maps, which receives that location. If you tap the map button, the app opens Google Maps at the place, and Google receives it under its own privacy policy.
- **TestFlight.** If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports and usage information and shares them with us, together with any feedback you send through TestFlight. If we invite you by email, we give TestFlight your name and email address. We use all of this only to run the beta and fix the app. Apple explains it in [TestFlight & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

Data from Apple Health and from the motion sensors is never used for advertising and never sold.

## Sessions you send us during the beta

During the closed beta we ask testers to export sessions and send them to us. Sending them is your choice.

- We use them only to learn how the app works for you: how often you train and how well it recognises your swings.
- We don't sell them, share them with anyone, or use them for advertising.
- We delete them when the closed beta ends, keeping only totals that identify no one. You can ask us to delete yours at any time before that.

## Your choices

- Change the app's access to Motion & Fitness, Health and Location at any time in Settings on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in the Health app.
- Delete sessions in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on that device. Workouts saved to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

## Changes

When the app starts to record, ask for or send something new, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
